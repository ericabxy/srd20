### Matis Traits

* Matis receive a +2 racial bonus to Intelligence and a --2 racial penalty to Strength.

* Matis base land speed is 30 feet.

* Medium: As Medium creatures, matis have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* Weapon Proficiency: Matis are automatically proficient with the longsword, rapier, longbow (including composite longbow), and shortbow (including composite shortbow).

* +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (history) checks: Matis pride themselves on being the keepers of much lore that has been forgotten to others.

* +2 racial bonus on any one Craft skill (chosen by the character at the time of creation).
