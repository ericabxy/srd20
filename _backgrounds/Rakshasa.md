## Rakshasa

### Rakshasa Traits

* Rakshasa gain a +2 racial bonus to Dexterity and a --2 racial penalty to Wisdom.

* Rakshasa base speed is 30 feet.

* __Medium:__ As Medium creatures, rakshasa have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* __Low-Light Vision.__ Rakshasa can see twice as far as normal in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. They retain the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions.

* +2 racial bonus on Intimidate, Search, Spot, Listen, and Survival.

* In place of gaining a feat as normal at 3rd level or any subsequent level, a rakshasa may instead gain [Bite](/Bite) as a bonus feat.

This background is based on the [SRD rakshasa](MonstersO-R#rakshasa) and the [litorian](Litorian) from _Arcana Unleashed_.
