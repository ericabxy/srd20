**Desert Dwarf Traits (Ex):** Desert dwarves possess the following racial traits.

— --2 Dexterity, +2 Constitution: Desert dwarves are tough enough to survive the brutal environment of the desert, but they rely more on cunning and fast talk than agility.

—Medium size.

—A desert dwarf ’s base land speed is 20 feet. However, desert dwarves can move at this speed even when wearing medium or heavy armor or when carrying a medium or heavy load.

* Heat Endurance: Desert dwarves gain a +4 racial bonus on Fortitude saves made to resist the effects of hot weather.

—Darkvision out to to 60 feet.

—Weapon Familiarity: Desert dwarves treat dwarven waraxes and dwarven urgroshes as martial weapons, rather than exotic weapons.

—Stability: Desert dwarves are exceptionally stable on their feet. A desert dwarf has a +4 bonus on ability checks made to resist being bull rushed or tripped when standing on the ground (but not when climbing, flying, riding, or otherwise not standing firmly on the ground).

— +2 racial bonus on saving throws against poison.

— +2 racial bonus on saving throws against spells and spell-like effects.

— +1 racial bonus on attack rolls against humanoids of the reptilian subtype (including kobolds and lizardfolk) and dragons (including half-dragons): Desert dwarves are trained in special combat techniques that allow them to fight common enemies more effectively.

— +4 dodge bonus to Armor Class against dragons: Dragons are a common threat in desert climes, and desert dwarves have adapted to meet this challenge.

— +2 racial bonus on Appraise checks that are related to stone or metal items.

• +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (architecture and engineering), Knowledge (dungeoneering), and profession (miner) checks: Desert dwarves protect a great deal of information about what lives beneath the desert and how to reach it.
