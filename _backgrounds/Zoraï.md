### Zoraï Traits

* Zoraï receive a +2 racial bonus to Dexterity and a --2 racial penalty to Constitution.

* Zoraï base land speed is 30 feet.

* Medium: As Medium creatures, zoraï have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* Weapon Proficiency: Zoraï are automatically proficient with the handaxe, rapier, shortsword, and shortbow (including composite shortbow).

* +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (nature) checks.
