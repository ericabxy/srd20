---
title: Locathah
---

--- Humanoid (aquatic): A locathah is a humanoid creature with the aquatic subtype. An aquatic creature can breathe underwater. A locathah can also breathe air because it has the amphibious special quality (see below).

--- Amphibious: A locathah can survive out of the water indefinitely.

--- Medium-size: As Medium creatures, locathah have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

--- Base land speed 30 feet.

--- Swim speed 30 feet. A locathah can move through water at its swim speed without making Swim checks. It has a +8 racial bonus on any Swim check to perform some action or avoid a hazard. It can always choose to take 10 on a Swim check, even if distracted or endangered. It can use the run action while swimming, provided it swims in a straight line.

--- Low-Light Vision: A locathah can see twice as far as normal in starlight, moonlight, torchlight, and similar conditions of poor illumination. They retain the ability to distinguish color and detail under these conditions.

--- Weapon Familiarity: Locathah are automatically proficient with longspears, tridents, light crossbows, and nets.
