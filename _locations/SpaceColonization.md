---
title: Space Colonization
---

The implications of a star system-spanning civilization are not trivial. Although the Solar Regions setting is meant to be a narrative-first resource---not realistic or morally sound---it worth considering the realism of closed ecological systems and the morality of space colonization.

For example, the [Ashen Reach](AshenReach) is fully a science-fantasy concept. Inhabitants of [Ione](Ione) and their visitors enjoy a perfectly breathable atmosphere along with prehistoric creatures like dinosaurs, and gravity is treated as completely normal. Natural resources are assumed to be transmuted at a sustainable rate from the Ionian lithosphere and atmosphere to sustain something on the level of a pre-industrial American frontier destination. While everything "just works" its also a good starting point to explore sustainable energy and material practices, right and wrong ways to approach community management and natural stewardship, et cetera.
