# The Ashen Reach

The Ashen Reach is part of a geothermal belt in the Median Region of Ione. It spans from the northern coast of the Great Sea from the Singing Spires in the west to the lava fields and the southernmost ranges of Estan Mons in the east. These coastal lowlands are bordered in the north by the first ranges of Estan Mons.

### The Northern Lands

The peaks and ranges further north in the Estan Mons are not maintained as habitable, so few people live or journey in that direction. Yet much of oral and written history in the Reach speaks of another body of water beyond the mountains known as the North Sea.

### The Northwest People

To the north and west stretch hundreds of kilometers of uninhabitable wilderness, but beyond it is known there are several communities in habitable bubbles much like the Ashen Reach. The details known by locals are sparse, but these near-fabled pockets of civilization are considered unseen sisters in the Ionian Earth-shaping project. Among those mentioned in written and oral history are the Scythians, the Cimmerians, and the massive land of [Hyperboreas](Hyperboreas).
