# Hyperboreas

Hyperboreas is a relatively large habitable region on Ione maintained by some of the most extensive and advanced Earth-shaping machines and processes. It is named after Rober E. Howard's [_Hyborian Age_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hyborian_Age) and it is encouraged to develop [_sword and sorcery_](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sword_and_sorcery) campaigns and stories set here.

Hyperboreas is completely undeveloped and unspecified. It is rumored to exist somewhere northwest of the [Ashen Reach](AshenReach), but its actual location is undetermined. It is simply an Ionian setting designed to accommodate more traditional heroic fantasy stories.
