# Ancient Rome (Historical Fantasy Version)

This fantasy setting for Srd20 takes place in a somewhat historically accurate Ancient Rome during its time as an expansive territory around the Mediterranean Sea. The main difference is the existence of magical technology and fantasy races standard to the SRD.

Scenarios and campaigns in Ancient Rome should focus on the cultural diversity of its territories and the local reality of Roman authority and infrastructure outside the capital. Rome's conquests assimilated cultures from many surrounding territories such as Gallia, Hibernia, Germania, etc..

Fantasy races such as elves, dwarves, and halflings live throughout Roman territory having spread throughout the world long before recorded history. Some examples are included in the scenario [_The Weight of Authority_](WeightOfAuthority); such as a [druidic scholar from Gaul](MachaExHibernia) with an elven lineage reaching back to ancient Hibernia, a [dwarven smith and mercenary](TignosNoricensis) from the mountain clans of Raetia, and a [germanic river-trader](LaufeyQuickstep) descended from a line of nomadic Scandanavian halflings.

## A.D. 125

During the reign of Emperor Hadrian, the Roman Empire had reached its greatest extent and was well into a golden age of [relative peace and stability](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pax_Romana). Anno Domini 125 is the year during which [_The Weight of Authority_](WeightOfAuthority) takes place in the province of [Noricum](Noricum) on the Roman frontier.
