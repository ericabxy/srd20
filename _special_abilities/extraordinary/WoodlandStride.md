Woodland Stride
---------------

__Benefits:__ You may move through any sort of undergrowth (such as natural thorns, briars, overgrown areas, and similar terrain) at your normal speed and without taking damage or suffering any other impairment.

However, thorns, briars, and overgrown areas that are enchanted or magically manipulated to impede motion still affect you.

Notes
-----

Classes: Druid (2nd), ranger (7th)

Prestige Classes: Holt warden (2nd)
