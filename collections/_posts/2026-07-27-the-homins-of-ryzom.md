_The Saga of Ryzom_ is a massively-multiplayer online role-playing game taking place on a fictional planet called Atys. Atys is a massive wilderness with small pockets of civilization inhabited by four humanoid races collectively known as "homins".

Fyros
-----

Discipline and engineering, survival in hostile (desert) environs.

Themes: Soldiers, blacksmiths, frontier settlers, endurance.

Skills: Craft, intimidate.

Equipment: Artisan's tools, military kit, firestarter.

Bonuses: +2 circumstance bonus on checks involving military heirarchy, fortifications, organized armies.

### Fyros Traits

__Medium:__ As Medium creatures, fyros have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

Matis
-----

Themes: Nobility, artistry, diplomacy, botany.

* +2 racial bonus to Knowledge (nature) checks when identifying plants.

* +2 racial bonus to Diplomacy and Sense Motive skill checks.

### Matis Traits

__Medium:__ As Medium creatures, matis have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

Tryker
------

Themes: Traders, sailors, inventors.

Skills: Profession (sailor), Appraise.

Equipment: Navigator's tools, rope, trade goods.

__Riverfolk:__ Never become lost on navigable waterways. +2 circumstance bonus on rolls to locate merchants, ferrymen, or smugglers willing to do business.

__Abilities:__ +2 Dexterity, -2 Strength.

### Tryker Traits

__Small:__ Because they are Small creatures, all trykers gain a +1 size bonus to Armor Class and attack rolls, and a +4 size bonus to all Stealth checks. They must use smaller-sized weapons and equipment, and can lift and carry only three-quarters as much as a Medium character.

Zoraï
-----

Themes: Mysticism, philosophy, diplomacy.

Skills: Concentration, Knowledge (religion), Knowledge (nature).

Equipment: Ceremonial robes, divine focus, herbal spell components.

__Spiritual Insight:__ +2 circumstance bonus when interacting with religious communities or interpreting rituals.

__Abilities:__ +2 Intelligence, -2 Constitution.

### Zoraï Traits

__Medium:__ As Medium creatures, zoraï have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.
