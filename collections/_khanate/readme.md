---
title: Khanate
---

An experimental classless system with a deliberately flat, yet colorful choice of racial backgrounds. These backgrounds shall have no ability bonuses or penalties, nor will they define a favored class as some SRD races do. Distinctions like higher strength, greater spellcasting abilities, etc. will instead be defined by discreet genetic advantages that can't be represented by simple number tweaks.

Fyros
-----

The fyros are known as solders and martial craftsman, as well as settling on the frontiers of a planet and enduring in wilderness environments.

Fyros possess the following traits.

__Speed:__ Base land speed is 30 feet.

Matis
-----

Thematically the matis are associated with nobility, art, diplomacy, and a cultural background in botany.

__Speed:__ Base land speed is 30 feet.

__Medium:__ No special bonuses or penalties due to size.

Tryker
------

The main physical distinction of the tryker racial background is an inherently small size, granting attack and armor bonuses but a penalty to speed and grapple. Genetic bonuses must be considered to offset the drawbacks.

Thematically the tryker are known for their cultural skills in trade, watercraft, and advanced mechanical engineering when it comes to devices.

Trykers possess the following background traits.

__Small:__ As a Small creature, you gain a +1 size bonus to Armor Class, a +1 size bonus on attack rolls, and a +4 size bonus on Hide checks, but you use smaller weapons than a Medium character, and your lifting and carrying limits are three-quarters of those of a Medium character.

__Speed:__ Base land speed is 20 feet.

--- +2 racial bonus on Craft (any), Disable Device, and Open Lock checks.

Zoraï
-----

The zoraï are known as mystics and theologians. Genetically they have a deeper connection to spirits and the diving forces of nature.

Zorai possess the following background traits.

__Medium:__ No special bonuses or penalties due to size.

__Speed:__ Base land speed is 30 feet.

--- +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (religion) and Knowledge (nature) checks.
