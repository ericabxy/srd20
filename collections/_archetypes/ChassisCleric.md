Chassis Cleric
--------------

__Hit Die:__ d8.

__Good Saving Throws:__ Fortitude and Will.

__Base Attack Bonus:__ 3/4 class level.

__Skills:__ 2 + Int modifier.

__Weapon and Armor Proficiency:__ The chassis cleric is proficient in the use of all simple and martial weapons and with light armor.
