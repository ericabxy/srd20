_The Solar Regions_ is a larger campaign setting which includes _The Outer Sky of Iovis_ and other potential locations such as _The Ocean Moon_ and _The Forest of Titan_. It takes place in a time and reality wherein myriad species and cultures have inhabited the planets, satellites, and other celestial bodies of the _Solar System_ for thousands of years.

Races and cultures in the _Solar Regions_ are countless and infinitely variable, but a small set of common species are available to select from for standard character backgrounds.

__[Blue](/Blue):__ A diminutive simian species with bat-like faces and bodies covered in a fine coat of fur. Despite the name, blue fur grows in a variety of hues due to interbreeding between the namesake species and the more varied goblinoids of the Inner and Outer Solar System.

__[Dromite](/Dromite):__ Small insectiod creatures descended from a species adapted to the environments of barren satellites and asteroids.

__[Elan](/Elan):__ A humanoid species known for their ethereal appearence due to their pale skin of blues, greens, grays, and other hues, and their often brightly colored eyes.

__[Half-giant](/Half-giant):__ A species of imposingly large humanoids believed by many scholars to be descended from the progenitors of intelligent life in the Solar System.

__[Maenad](/Maenad):__ A species of average-sized humanoids with an ability to perform feats of strength beyond their physical capacity for short lengths of time.

__[Xeph](/Xeph):__ A graceful species of humanoids known for resistance to magical effects and capable of exhibiting temporary bursts of speed.
