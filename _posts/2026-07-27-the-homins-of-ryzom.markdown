_The Saga of Ryzom_ is a massively-multiplayer online role-playing game taking place on a fictional planet called Atys. Atys is a massive wilderness with small pockets of civilization inhabited by four humanoid peoples collectively known as "homins".


Fyros
-----

The [Fyros](https://en.wiki.ryzom.com/wiki/Fyros) are a bold warrior civilization who boast the values of truth, honesty, and discipline. They are determined and fierce, demonstrating their hardiness by living on the frontiers of the Burning Desert. 

### Fyros Traits

* Fyros are a gruff, hardy people adapted to life in the harsh wilderness of Atys. They gain a +2 racial bonus to Constitution and a --2 racial penalty to Charisma.

* Fyros base land speed is 30 feet.

* Medium: As Medium creatures, fyros have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* Weapon Proficiency: Fyros receive the Martial Weapon Proficiency feats for the scimitar, rapier, and shortbow (including composite shortbow) as bonus feats.

* +2 racial bonus on Craft (any weapon or armor) checks.

* +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (military) checks.

* Endurance: Fyros are adept at surviving in desert and other wilderness environments, receiving Endurance as a racial bonus feat.


Matis
-----

The [Matis](https://en.wiki.ryzom.com/wiki/Matis) are a proud people descending from the rulers of a lost kingdom. Their culture prides itself on refined artistic tastes and noble purpose, and they are well versed in the art of botany.

### Matis Traits

* Matis receive a +2 racial bonus to Intelligence and a --2 racial penalty to Strength.

* Matis base land speed is 30 feet.

* Medium: As Medium creatures, matis have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge checks when identifying plants and Craft checks related to botany.

* +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (history) checks: Matis culture emphasizes the preservation of knowledge pertaining to the history of Atys and its peoples.

* +2 racial bonus on Diplomacy and Sense Motive skill checks.


Tryker
------

The [Tryker](https://en.wiki.ryzom.com/wiki/Tryker) are a people of short stature with a flighty and carefree culture. They are known as explorers and inventors, as experts in wind technology, and for building large floating cities on the Lakelands.

Themes: Traders, sailors, inventors.

### Tryker Traits

* The Tryker are physically small, yet nimble homins receiving a +2 racial bonus to Dexterity and a --2 racial penalty to Strength.

* Tryker base land speed is 20 feet.

* Small: Because they are Small creatures, all trykers gain a +1 size bonus to Armor Class and attack rolls, and a +4 size bonus to all Stealth checks. They must use smaller-sized weapons and equipment, and can lift and carry only three-quarters as much as a Medium character.

* +2 racial bonus on Climb and Swim checks: Trykers are born and raised in cities built over lakes. They live their lives in and out of the water and clambering in and out of boats.

* +2 racial bonus on Craft (shipbuilding) checks: Trykers are trained from an early age to maintain and construct watercraft of all sorts.

* +2 racial bonus on Use Rope checks: Ropecraft is ubiquitous in tryker culture, being an indispensable tool in shipcraft and marine navigation.


Zoraï
-----

The [Zoraï](https://en.wiki.ryzom.com/wiki/Zoraï) are from a culture of mystics who zealousy serve the living planet of Atys. Their search for spiritual enlightenment have earned them an affinity with the mysterious spirits of the plant kingdom.

### Zoraï Traits

* Zoraï receive a +2 racial bonus to Dexterity and a --2 racial penalty to Constitution.

* Zoraï base land speed is 30 feet.

* Medium: As Medium creatures, zoraï have no special bonuses or penalties due to their size.

* +2 racial bonus on Knowledge (religion) checks.

* +2 circumstance bonus on Knowledge (nature) checks involving nature spirits, deities, and customs.
