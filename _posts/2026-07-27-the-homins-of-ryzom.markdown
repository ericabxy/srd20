_The Saga of Ryzom_ is a massively-multiplayer online role-playing game taking place on a fictional planet called Atys.

Atys is a massive wilderness with small pockets of civilization inhabited by four humanoid peoples collectively known as "homins". While SRD races have traits reflecting a long history of game design, _Ryzom_ has no tabletop RPG precedents so the backgrounds created for this project have only a few very obvious specializations each.


Fyros
-----

The [Fyros](/Fyros) are a bold warrior civilization who boast the values of truth, honesty, and discipline. They are determined and fierce, demonstrating their hardiness by living on the frontiers of the Burning Desert. 


Matis
-----

The [Matis](/Matis) are a proud people descending from the rulers of a lost kingdom. Their culture prides itself on refined artistic tastes and noble purpose, and they are well versed in the art of botany.


Tryker
------

The [Tryker]/Tryker) are a people of short stature with a flighty and carefree culture. They are known as explorers and inventors, as experts in wind technology, and for building large floating cities on the Lakelands.


Zoraï
-----

The [Zoraï](/Zoraï) are from a culture of mystics who zealousy serve the living planet of Atys. Their search for spiritual enlightenment have earned them an affinity with the mysterious spirits of the plant kingdom.
