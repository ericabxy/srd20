Information for the campaign setting _The Outer Sky of Iovis_ is being copied to the generic `locations` and `characters` collections for further development. Originally conceived as a collection of artifacts, I've decided it will work better as part of the overall SRD Project with hyperlinks cross-referencing each part of the setting.

The [_Outer Sky_](/OuterSky) is a region of space around Iovis wherein the moon [Ione](/Ione) and associated objects orbit the planet. The surface of Ione is home to dozens of partially-terraformed habitable regions such as the [Ashen Reach](/AshenReach).
